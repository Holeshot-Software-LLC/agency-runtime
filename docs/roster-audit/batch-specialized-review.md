# Roster semantic safety audit - specialized and spatial batch

## Result

This batch semantically reviewed the complete prompt body of **95 source
agents** at upstream revision `459dce837db3bdfdc4763d3fefd1fd854e73c8f1`. The review covered
**27,045 lines** and **1,398,400 source bytes**. The machine-readable
record contains exactly one entry per source file and does not copy prompt bodies.

- Approved under the bounded runtime contract: **95**
- Quarantined: **0**
- Direct-context safe after projection: **15**
- Isolated-context only: **80**
- Authority projection: advise 8, modify 32, plan 24, review 31

"Approved" does not approve every raw directive or embedded example. It means the
useful specialty remains selectable only through the bounded routing contract.
Raw claims of memory, tool access, executive or professional authority, external
action, certification, legal currency, or production readiness remain inactive.

Audit revision 2 also records read-only `analysis` for `lsp-index-engineer`.
That identifier is grounded in its reviewed graph-consistency, profiling,
cancellation, stale-file, duplicate-symbol, hostile-input, and benchmark evidence
contracts. It permits bounded diagnosis before an implementation decision; it does
not grant independent review authority, host mutation, global tool installation, or
an unsupported performance claim.

## Conflict and composition decisions

The audit records 5 symmetric conflict pairs where raw governing
methods would compete if loaded into the same context:

- `accounts-payable-agent ↔ chief-financial-officer`
- `automation-governance-architect ↔ workflow-architect`
- `cultural-intelligence-strategist ↔ korean-business-navigator`
- `data-privacy-officer ↔ identity-graph-operator`
- `organizational-psychologist ↔ personal-growth-mentor`

These represent autonomous payment versus executive financial control, vendor-first
automation versus implementation-neutral workflow design, automated identity
consolidation versus privacy minimization, workplace coaching versus psychological
assessment boundaries, and cultural humility versus universalized country rules.
They may be routed as isolated adversarial reviews, but must not co-govern one
context without an explicit conflict decision.

Concrete dependencies are:

- `data-consolidation-agent → sales-data-extraction-agent`
- `report-distribution-agent → data-consolidation-agent`
- `solution-engineer → technical-consultant`

The resident-manager kernel keeps Agents Orchestrator and Chief of Staff present
together outside ordinary specialist dependency closure, so their roster contracts
must not encode a reciprocal `requires` cycle. Solution engineering requires
strategy framing, data consolidation requires validated extraction, and distribution
requires a reviewed consolidated report. A raw prompt naming another persona does
not otherwise self-activate it.

The 15 nested game-development profiles add no hard conflict or dependency edge.
Their engine, art, design, networking, and tooling overlaps are composition
opportunities, not proof that one persona must self-activate or co-govern another.
All new `conflicts_with` and `requires` lists are empty; the existing five conflict
pairs remain symmetric and all five dependency edges resolve within the batch.
