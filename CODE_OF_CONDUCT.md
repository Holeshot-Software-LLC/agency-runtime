# Code of Conduct

Agency Runtime welcomes constructive participation from people with different
backgrounds, experience levels, identities, and perspectives. Project spaces
should remain useful, safe, and focused on improving the software.

## Expected conduct

- Be respectful, specific, and patient when discussing code or decisions.
- Critique ideas and implementations without attacking people.
- Share enough evidence for others to reproduce a claim, while protecting
  credentials, private prompts, personal information, and security details.
- Respect maintainer decisions about scope, disclosure timing, and release
  claims; disagreement may continue through documented technical reasoning.
- Help make issues, reviews, documentation, and support accessible to people
  who are new to the project.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention,
deliberate disruption, and publication of another person's private information
are not acceptable in any project space.

## Reporting and enforcement

For ordinary conduct concerns, contact the maintainers through a private
organizational channel. For vulnerabilities or reports containing sensitive
technical material, use the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
Do not open a public issue containing private evidence.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, limit participation, or ban an
account when needed to protect the community or project. Enforcement should be
proportionate, documented privately, and applied consistently. A report made
in good faith must not be retaliated against.
